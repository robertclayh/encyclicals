PASTORAL VISIT OF THE HOLY FATHER LEO XIVTO PAVIA AND SANT'ANGELO LODIGIANO

20 JUNE 2026

[Multimedia]

_____________________________

13.00 Departure from the Vatican heliport

14.45 Arrival at the Rugby CUS Field in Cravino.

The Holy Father will be welcomed by:

1. Bishop Corrado Sanguineti of Pavia

2. The Honourable Attilio Fontana, President of the Lombardy Region

3. Dr. Francesca De Carlini, Prefect of Pavia

4. Dr. Michele Lissia, Mayor of Pavia

5. Dr. Giovanni Palli, President of the Province of Pavia.

Immediate transfer by car to the National Centre for Oncological Hadrontherapy (CNAO).

15.00 At the entrance of the CNAO, the Holy Father will be welcomed by:

1. Dr. Gianluca Vago, President

2. Dr. Sandro Rossi, Director.

The Holy Father will meet and greet various managers, medical staff, and some children undergoing treatment, accompanied by their parents.

15.30 The Holy Father will leave the CNAO and transfer by car to the Basilica ofSan Pietroin Ciel d’Oro

15.45 Convent of the Augustinian Fathers.

The Holy Father will be welcomed by:

1. Father Joseph L. Farrell, Prior General

2. Father Gabriele Pedicino, Provincial Prior

3. Father Gianfranco Casagrande, Prior of the Convent.

Brief meeting with the Augustinian Community.

In the Cloister: greeting to the bishops of Lombardy and some collaborators.

16.15 The bishops, clergy, consecrated men and women and permanent deacons of Lombardy will be present in the Basilica.

- Greeting from Bishop Corrado Sanguineti.

Celebration of the Word and veneration of the relics of Saint Augustine.

*Address of the Holy Father

17.00 The Holy Father will leave the Basilica and greet the faithful in the adjacent square.

Transfer by car to Piazza Duomo.

On the parvis of the Cathedral, the Holy Father will receive a floral tribute from some children, and will greet the children and the South American community present in the Square.

The Holy Father will enter the Cathedral, welcomed by members of the Chapter; adoration of the Blessed Sacrament and pause before the altar of Saint Syrus, first bishop and patron of the diocese and the city.

17.15 The Holy Father will leave the Cathedral and proceed to Piazza Vittoria on foot.

17.30 Piazza Vittoria: meeting with the citizenry:

- greeting from Dr. Michele Lissia, Mayor of Pavia

- greeting from Bishop Corrado Sanguineti.

*Address of the Holy Father

At the end, the Holy Father will greet a representation of authorities and faithful.

18.30 The Holy Father will leave Piazza Vittoria and proceed to the Rugby CUS Field of Cravino, where he will bid farewell to the authorities who welcomed him upon arrival.

18.45 Departure from Pavia.

________________

18.55 Arrival at the “Carlo Chiesa” municipal Stadium in Via Francesco Cortese.

The Holy Father will be welcomed by:

1. Bishop Maurizio Malvestiti of Lodi

2. Dr. Cristiano Devecchi, Mayor of Sant’Angelo Lodigiano.

Transfer by car to the parish ofSanti Antonio Abate e Francesca Cabrini, where he will be welcomed by the parish priest, Msgr. Enzo Raimondi.

In the Church: adoration of the Blessed Sacrament and veneration of the Heart of Saint Francesca Cabrini.

- greeting from Bishop Maurizio Malvestiti.

*Address of the Holy Father

19.30 The Holy Father will leave the parish and transfer to the communal stadium, where he will bid farewell to the authorities who welcomed him upon arrival.

19.45 Departure from Sant’Angelo Lodigiano

21.15 Arrival at the Vatican heliport.
