VIDEO MESSAGE OF THE HOLY FATHER FRANCISTO PARTICIPANTS IN THELAUDATO SI' INTER-UNIVERSITY CONGRESS

[ARGENTINA, 1-4 SEPTEMBER 2021]

I greet the members of theLaudato si'Inter-University Congress, to take place from 1 to 4 September.

I hope that this effort will advance social awareness and consciousness for the care of our common home. The EncyclicalLaudato si'is not only a "green" Encyclical, it is also a "social" Encyclical. I hope that this Congress will help its full scope and all its consequences to be seen. I wish you all the best, God bless you and do not forget to pray for me. Thank you.
