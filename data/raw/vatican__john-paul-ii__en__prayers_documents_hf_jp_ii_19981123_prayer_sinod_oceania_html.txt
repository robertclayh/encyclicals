Eternal God,look with favour upon us,as we draw our strengthfrom the Special Assemblyof the Synod of Bishops for Oceaniato welcome the new millenniumin Jesus Christ.

He is our Word become fleshwho invites us to walk his way, to tellhis truth and to live his life.

We, the peoples of Oceania,diverse in our ways,and scattered over a large area,pledge to proclaim his loveto the world, to search for thosegone astray and to lead them to you.

Mary, Queen of Peace,intercede for us your children,as we make this prayer in Jesus' name.

Amen.
