POPE FRANCIS

MORNING MEDITATION IN THE CHAPEL OF THEDOMUS SANCTAE MARTHAE

Celebrating with Vatican gardeners and cleaners

Friday, 22 March 2013

(byL'Osservatore Romano, Weekly ed. in English, n. 13, 27 March 2013)

When we have a heart of stone it happens that we pick up real stones and stone Jesus Christ in the person of our brothers and sisters, especially the weakest of them. Pope Francis said this at the Mass he celebrated on Friday morning, 22 March, in the Chapel of theDomus Sanctae Marthae. Various employees of the garden and cleaning services of the Governorate of Vatican City State were invited to the celebration. In his Homily, the Holy Father spoke about the passage in the Gospel of John in which the Jews wanted to stone Jesus. Also present were sisters of three communities of women religious who work in Vatican City: those who work in the Santa Marta Paediatric Dispensary, in the home for retired apostolic nuncios, and in the Holy Father’s private storeroom.
