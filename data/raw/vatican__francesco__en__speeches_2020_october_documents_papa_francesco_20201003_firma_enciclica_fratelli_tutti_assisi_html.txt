VISIT OF THE HOLY FATHER FRANCIS TO ASSISI:HOLY MASS AND SIGNING OF THE NEW ENCYCLICAL“ALL BROTHERS”, ON FRATERNITY AND SOCIAL FRIENDSHIP

REMARKSOF THE HOLY FATHER

Basilica of Saint Francis of AssisiSaturday, 3 October 2020

[Multimedia]

I will now sign the Encyclical, which will be brought to the altar by Msgr Paolo Braida, who is responsible for translations and also the Pope’s discourses at the First Section [of the Secretariat of State]. He oversees everything and this is why I wanted him to be present here, today, and to bring me the Encyclical. Two translators have come with him: Fr Antonio, translator in Portuguese, who translated from Spanish to Portuguese, and Fr Cruz, who is Spanish, and who oversaw the other translations from the Spanish original. I do so as a sign of gratitude to the entire First Section of the Secretariat of State, which has worked on the draft and translation.

[He then signs the Encyclical]

They are humble, these translators; they hide themselves away!

*L'Osservatore Romano, Weekly edition in english, n.41, 09/10/2020,
