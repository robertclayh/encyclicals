RESCRIPTUM EX AUDIENTIA SS.MI

After considering that

- The Apostolic ConstitutionPastor Bonus, of 28 June 1988, inspired by the Magisterium ofVatican Council II(cf. Decree on the pastoral mission of bishops in the ChurchChristus Dominis, of 28 October 1965, no. 16), attributed to the Pontifical Council for the Pastoral Care of Migrants and Itinerant Peoples the competence both of the pastoral care of exiles, migrants, nomads, circus people, seafarers and air transport workers (cf. art. 150 Pastor Bonus), and of the spiritual care of travellers (cf. art. 151PastorBonus);

- By the Motu ProprioHumanam progressionem, of 17 August 2016, the competences of the aforementioned Pontifical Council were transferred to theDicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development;

- By the Motu ProprioSanctuarium in Ecclesia, of 11 February 2017, certain competences under the aforementioned Article 151 ofPastor Bonuswere transferred to the Pontifical Council for Promoting New Evangelization;

- The Apostolic ConstitutionPraedicate Evangelium, of 19 March 2022, requested a redistribution of the aforementioned competences,

The Holy Father Francis, in the audience granted to the undersigned Prefect of the Dicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development, on the day 7 September 2022, provided for the transfer of the competence for the pastoral care of faithful who undertake journeys for reasons of piety or study or leisure from the same Dicastery to the Section for fundamental questions of evangelization in the world of the Dicastery for Evangelization.

This Rescript will come into effect on 1 October 2022, by publication inL’Osservatore Romano, and then in theActa Apostolicae Sedis.

From the Vatican, 30 September 2022

Cardinal Michael Czerny, S.J.Prefect
