GREETING OF THE HOLY FATHERTO A TAOIST DELEGATION FROM TAIWAN

Room adjacent to Paul VI Audience HallWednesday, 14 March 2018

[Multimedia]

I thank you very much for your visit and for your words. I am pleased about this Joint Work with the Pontifical Council for Interreligious Dialogue. It is a dialogue not only of ideas; it is a human dialogue, from person to person, that helps everyone to grow, to grow as people, in our journey in search of the absolute, of God. Thank you very much, thank you for your good will. Thank you for your visit and thank you for your invitation to visit Taiwan. I greatly appreciate all of this.

May the Lord bless you all, and pray for me.
