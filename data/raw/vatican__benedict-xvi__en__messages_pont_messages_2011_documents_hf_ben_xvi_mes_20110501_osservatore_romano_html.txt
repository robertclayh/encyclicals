MESSAGE OF HIS HOLINESS BENEDICT XVITO THE READERS OF L'OSSERVATORE ROMANOON THE OCCASION OF THE BEATIFICATION OF POPE JOHN PAUL II

I extend to the readers ofL'Osservatore Romano, to the faithful of Rome and to the pilgrims who have come from all over the world my most cordial welcome on the day of theBeatification of Pope John Paul II. May this celebration of faith be a precious opportunity to open the doors to Christ and a pressing invitation to live, with the new Blessed’s generosity, the Gospel of Love.

May everyone receive my Blessing.

From the Vatican, 1 May 2011

BENEDICTUS PP. XVI
