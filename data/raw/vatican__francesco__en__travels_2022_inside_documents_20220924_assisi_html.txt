VISIT OF THE HOLY FATHER FRANCISTO ASSISIFOR THE EVENT “ECONOMY OF FRANCESCO”SATURDAY, 24 SEPTEMBER 2022

Saturday, 24 September 2022

The Holy Father will transfer by car to thePalaEventi, where he will be welcomed by:

- three young people, representing the youths participating in the event;- Cardinal Michael Czerny, prefect of the Dicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development;- Archbishop Domenico Sorrentino, bishop of Assisi-Nocera Umbra-Gualdo Tadino, and of Foligno;- Dr. Donatella Tesei, president of the Umbria Region;- Dr. Armando Gradone, prefect of Perugia;- Dr. Stefania Proietti, mayoress of Assisi and president of the Province of Perugia;- Members of the Organizing Committee of the Event;- Professor Luigino Bruni;- Dr. Francesca di Maolo;- Sr. Alessandra Smerilli;

- Representatives of the Franciscan Families of Assisi and ofPro Civitate Christiana.

The meeting with young people will take place as follows:

- artistic-theatrical moment;- welcome and introduction;- eight young people recount their experiences.

* Address of the Holy Father

- reading and signing of the “Pact”;

- the Holy Father will greet the young people present onstage.

Bulletin of the Holy See Press Office, 8 July 2022
