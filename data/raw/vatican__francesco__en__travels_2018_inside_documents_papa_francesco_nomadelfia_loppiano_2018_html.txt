TO NOMADELFIA, IN THE DIOCESE OF GROSSETO,
TO MEET THE COMMUNITY FOUNDED BY DON ZENO SALTINI;
AND TO LOPPIANO (FLORENCE) IN THE DIOCESE OF FIESOLE, TO VISIT THE
INTERNATIONAL CENTRE OF THE  FOCOLARE MOVEMENT
10 MAY 2018

NOMADELFIA
- Visit to the Community founded by Don Zeno Saltini (1900-1981)
in the province and diocese of Grosseto
