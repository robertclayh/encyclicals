ADDRESS OF POPE LEO XIVTO MEMBERS OF THE AUSILIA FOUNDATION

Hall of PopesMonday, 30 March 2026

[Multimedia]

______________________________

Your Eminence,Your Excellencydear brothers and sisters,

I am pleased to greet you this morning at the start ofHoly Week, a providential time for our discipleship of the Lord Jesus, who was crucified, died and rose again for us.

Thank you for your generous commitment to helping young people in their formation and integration into the workplace. Investing not in objects, but in people, in their abilities and skills, constitutes a key strength of your work. Indeed, in this way, it is the young people themselves who become the protagonists of their own future, without being regarded as functional tools for a company or cogs in the wheel of commercial success.

I therefore encourage every member of theAusiliaFoundation to persevere in the initiatives you are fostering: precisely in the current climate of uncertainty, the virtuous circle of knowledge and collaboration that you promote will certainly bear good fruit.

As a sign of esteem, I gladly impart my Apostolic Blessing upon all of you here present, and upon your loved ones. Thank you!
