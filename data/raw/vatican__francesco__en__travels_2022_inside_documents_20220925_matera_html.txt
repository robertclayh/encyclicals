PASTORAL VISIT OF THE HOLY FATHER FRANCISTO MATERAFOR THE CONCLUSION OF THE 27th NATIONAL EUCHARISTIC CONGRESS

SUNDAY, 25 SEPTEMBER 2022

Sunday, 25 September 2022

Transfer by car to the municipal stadium.

The Holy Father will be received by:

- Cardinal Matteo Maria Zuppi, archbishop of Bologna, president of the Italian Episcopal Conference;- Archbishop Antonio Giuseppe Caiazzo of Matera-Irsina;- The Hon. Vito Bardi, president of the Basilicata region;- Dr. Sante Capponi, prefect of Matera;- Dr. Domenico Bennardi, mayor of Matera;- Dr. Piero Marrese, president of the province of Matera.

*Homily of the Holy Father*Angelus

- Words of thanks from Cardinal Matteo Maria Zuppi, archbishop of Bologna, president of the Italian Episcopal Conference.

The Pope gives a chalice for the diocese of Matera to Archbishop Antonio Giuseppe Caiazzo.

Transfer by car to the School Athletics Field.
