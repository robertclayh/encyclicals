CHIROGRAPH OF THE HOLY FATHER FRANCIS

Having considered the request of the Venerable Brother Cardinal Baltazar E. Porras Cardozo, Apostolic Administrator of the Archdiocese of Caracas, Venezuela, by Letter No. AAC/26-21, of  9 March 2021;

having accepted the petition of the Rector Magnificus of the Pontifical Lateran University;

I willingly dispose

that the Venerable Servant of God José Gregorio Hernández Cisneros, Lay Faithful, born on 26 October 1864 in Isnotú, Venezuela, and died on 29 June 1919 in Caracas, Venezuela, whose Beatification was established in the Archdiocese of Caracas on 30 April 2021, be madeCo-Patronof theCycle of Studies in Peace Sciencesinstituted by me at the Pontifical Lateran University with the LetterThe Desire for Peaceof 12 November 2018 (inAAS, CX [2018], 1720-1722), placing him alongside Blessed John of Peace.

Franciscus

L'Osservatore Romano,Weekly Edition in English, 7 May 2021
