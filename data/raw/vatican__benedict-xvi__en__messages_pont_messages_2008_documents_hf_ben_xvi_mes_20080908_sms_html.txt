SMS MESSAGE BY HIS HOLINESS BENEDICT XVITO THE YOUNG PEOPLEFIFTY DAYS AFTER THE CELEBRATION OF MASSON THE OCCASION OF WORLD YOUTH DAY 2008

Dear Friends,

Fifty days ago we were together for the celebration of Mass.Today I greet you on the birthday of Mary, Mother of the Church.Empowered by the Spirit and courageous like Mary your pilgrimage of faithfills the Church with life!Soon I am to visit France.I ask you all to join me in praying for the young people of France.May we all be rejuvenated in hope!

From Castel Gandolfo, 8 September 2008.

BENEDICTUS PP. XVI
