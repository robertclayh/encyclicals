MESSAGE OF THE HOLY FATHER PAUL VITO OLUSEGUN OBASANJO,PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF NIGERIA*

Thursday, 20 January 1977

To His ExcellencyGeneral Olusegun ObasanjoHead of State

On the happyoccasion of the opening of the Second World Black and African Festival of Arts and Culture, of which Your Excellency is Honorary President, we are happy to extend to you, to the People of Nigeria, to the President of the Festival and to all the representatives of the countries and peoples taking part, our cordial and prayerful good wishes for the success of this event of such significance for the affirmation and appreciation of the history and of the cultural values originating in the great African Continent.

From the Vatican, 10January 1977.

PAULUS PP. VI
