APOSTOLIC LETTERIN THE FORM OF A «MOTU PROPRIO»

OF POPE LEO XIV

IMMOTA MANET

ON THE CENTRAL SECTOR OF THE DIOCESE OF ROME

While the motivation, premises and considerations set out in the Motu ProprioLa vera bellezza, issued on 4 October 2024 bymy venerable Predecessor, remain unchanged, and considering that some reflections concerned preciselythe Jubilee Yearthat is about to end, in which, nevertheless, not only a specificity but also a homogeneity and unity of the Central Sector of the Diocese of Rome emerged even more clearly, with this Apostolic Letter in the form of a Motu Proprio, I establish and decree that the five Prefectures, from I to V, shall once again become part of a single Central Sector, which shall therefore be added once more to the other four Sectors of the Diocese of Rome.

I order that what has been decided in this Apostolic Letter shall have firm and stable force, notwithstanding anything to the contrary, even if worthy of special mention, and that it shall be promulgated by publication inL’Osservatore Romano, entering into force on the day of publication, and subsequently included in the official commentary of theActa Apostolicae Sedis.

Given in Rome, at Saint Peter’s, on 11 November 2025, Memorial of Saint Martin, first year of my Pontificate.

LEO PP. XIV
