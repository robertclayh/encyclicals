PASTORAL VISIT
OF HIS HOLINESS BENEDICT XVI
TO VIGEVANO AND PAVIA (ITALY)
21-22 APRIL 2007
PROGRAM
Saturday, 21 April 2007
15.15
Departure by car from Vatican City to Ciampino Airport
15.30
Departure by plane from Ciampino Airport to Milan/Linate Airport
16.30
Arrival in Milan-Linate Airport (Military Sector) and transfer by helicopter to Vigevano
Vigevano
16.50
Arrival at "Dante Merlo" stadium of Vigevano and transfer by "popemobile" to Piazza Ducale and Piazza Sant'Ambrogio
17.15
Arrival at the Bishopric. The Holy Father will greet the people from the central balcony
Greeting of the Holy Father and Blessing to youth and sick
17.30
Eucharistic Concelebration in Piazza Ducale with the Bishops of Lombardy and the priests of the diocese
Homily of the Holy Father
19.15
Transfer by car from the Bishopric to "Dante Merlo" stadium in Vigevano
19.30
Farewell to the public authorities.
19.45
Departure by helicopter for Pavia
Pavia
20.00
Arrival at "P. Fortunati" stadium in Pavia and transfer by car to downtown Pavia
20.15
Arrival at Piazza Duomo where the youth of the diocese are gathered
Address of the Holy Father
20.30
Farewell and overnight at the Bishopric of Pavia
Sunday, 22 April 2007
8.45
Transfer by car from the Bishopric to "San Matteo" Polyclinic
9.00
Arrival at "San Matteo" Polyclinic where the Holy Father will meet directors, medical staff, sick and their family members
Address of the Holy Father
9.45
Transfer from the Polyclinic to "Orti Borromaici" Esplanade " for Mass Celebration
10.30
Eucharistic Concelebration with the Bishops of Lombardy, the Priests of the Diocese and representatives of the Augustinian Fathers
Homily of the Holy Father
Regina Caeli
12.30
Transfer by car to the Bishopric
13.00
Luncheon with the Bishops of Lombardy
16.00
Greeting to the Bishop's family members and benefactors
16.15
Meeting with the world of culture at the  University's Theresian Courtyard in Pavia
Address of the Holy Father
17.00
Transfer to the Basilica of St. Pietro in Ciel d'Oro where the Holy Father will meet priests, men and women religious and seminarians
17.30
Liturgy of Vespers at the Basilica of St. Pietro in Ciel d'Oro
Homily of the Holy Father
18.30
Transfer to "P. Fortunati" stadium in Pavia and farewell to the public authorities. Departure by helicopter for Milan
19.00
Departure by plane for Rome-Ciampino Airport
20.15
Arrival at the Vatican City
